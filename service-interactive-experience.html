Interactive Experience

Product family 03 / 06

Allowing buyers to explore the development instead of only watching it.

(01)   The client challenge

Traditional films and brochures follow a fixed sequence.

Salespeople and buyers often need to jump instantly to a specific tower, amenity, floor, unit or view — and a linear asset cannot do that.

(02)   The Futé solution

Interactive experiences that let users navigate the project dynamically and explore information based on their own interest.

One connected model behind the interface, so a tower, a floor, a unit and the view from its balcony are all one click apart.

(03)   What you get

(05)   Case study

Challenge, solution,
result.

Placeholder narrative — replace with a named client story once approved.

  1. Client challenge

    A four-tower development had 11 unit types. Every sales conversation stalled while the executive hunted through a PDF for the right plan.

  2. Futé solution

    We built an interactive masterplan with tower, floor and unit selection, each unit linked to its plan, its finishes and the actual view from its balcony.

  3. Result & impact

    The plan hunt disappeared from the conversation. Sales staff answered "what can I see from there?" in one tap.
