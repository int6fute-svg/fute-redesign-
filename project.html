Axis Business District

A twelve-acre mixed-use district needed to be sold two years before its first tenant moved in. We built the whole precinct once, then drove the launch campaign from it.

(01)   The brief

Sell a district, not a building — show a place people would want to spend a day in, not just work in.

The developer's leasing team needed imagery that answered a specific objection: prospective tenants could see the floor plates, but nobody could picture the street life between the towers. Everything hinged on the podium.

We shifted the camera down to eye level for the hero frames, set the whole set at blue hour so the retail interiors read as warm against the glass, and populated the plaza with the density it would actually have at 7pm on a Thursday.
